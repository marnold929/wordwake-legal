# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how WordWake ("we," "our," or "the app") handles information when you use the WordWake iOS app and its Lock Screen widget.

WordWake is built with privacy as the default. We want this policy to be readable in two minutes.

## The short version

- **We do not have user accounts.** You do not create one, and we do not store one.
- **We do not collect personal information.** No name, email, location, phone number, contacts, or browsing history.
- **We do not run servers that store your data.** Everything WordWake needs to function lives on your device.
- **We do not sell, rent, or share any user data**, because we don't collect it in the first place.
- **We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.**

If something in WordWake feels confusing about privacy, please contact us — we will explain it plainly.

## What WordWake does on your device

WordWake stores the following information locally on your iPhone, using Apple's standard app and widget storage:

- Your selected language (e.g., Spanish, French)
- Your current stage and learning progress
- Your subscription status (Free or WordWake Plus)
- Settings such as which feeds are enabled

This information never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to us or to any third party. If you delete the WordWake app, this information is deleted with it.

## Apple In-App Purchases

WordWake Plus is sold as an in-app purchase through Apple. When you subscribe or purchase, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple. We receive an anonymous receipt from Apple that confirms whether you have an active subscription. We do not receive your name, email, billing address, or payment information.

Apple's handling of your purchase is governed by [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), not ours.

## Crash reports and diagnostics

If you have "Share With App Developers" enabled in your iPhone's privacy settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and basic performance data through Apple's standard developer tools. These reports do not contain personal information and we use them only to fix bugs.

You can turn this off at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements** on your iPhone.

We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, Mixpanel, Amplitude, or any other third-party analytics or advertising service.

## Children's privacy

WordWake is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. Because we do not collect personal information from anyone, this is true by design.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top and post the new version at this URL before the change takes effect.

# WordWake

A passive language-learning iOS app. Learn Spanish, French, German, Russian, or Italian on your Lock Screen.

Add the WordWake widget once, then quick language cards appear every time you wake your phone. No streaks. No homework. Just tiny moments of learning throughout your day.

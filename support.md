# WordWake Support

Welcome. This page covers the most common questions. If you don't find what you need, email us at **hello@magnetmates.app** — we read every message.

## Getting started

### How does WordWake actually work?

WordWake's main feature is a Lock Screen widget. Once you add the widget, your iPhone shows you a new language card each time iOS refreshes widgets. The app itself is just for setup, picking what shows up, and taking the occasional stage check.

### How do I add the WordWake widget to my Lock Screen?

1. Lock your iPhone, then wake it up.
2. Long-press the Lock Screen until the **Customize** button appears.
3. Tap **Customize**, then **Lock Screen**.
4. Tap the widget area below the clock.
5. Scroll to **WordWake** and tap it.
6. Tap **Done** in the top right.

If you get stuck, Apple has a step-by-step guide at https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/customize-the-lock-screen-iph4f49a3565/ios.

### How often does the widget change cards?

iOS controls when widgets refresh. We request frequent updates, but the actual frequency depends on your phone's battery state, your usage patterns, and iOS's own scheduling. In typical use, you'll see new cards multiple times per day.

## Subscriptions and purchases

### What's the difference between Free and WordWake Plus?

- **Free:** the Language feed, first 3 stages, in your chosen language.
- **Plus:** all 101 stages, all 5 launch languages (Spanish, French, German, Russian, Italian), plus three bonus feeds (Elite English vocabulary, Curious Facts, Sharp Thinking).

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. To cancel:

1. Open **Settings** on your iPhone
2. Tap your name at the top
3. Tap **Subscriptions**
4. Find **WordWake Plus** and tap **Cancel Subscription**

Or go directly: https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions

Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.

### How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open WordWake, go to **Settings**, and tap **Restore Purchases**. As long as you're signed in to the same Apple ID that made the original purchase, your access will be restored.

### I want a refund.

Refunds are processed by Apple, not by us. Visit https://reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, find your WordWake Plus purchase, and follow the prompts.

### Will I be charged when my free trial ends?

If you started a free trial, Apple will automatically charge for a paid subscription when the trial ends, unless you cancel before then. To cancel, follow the steps above.

## Using the app

### Why does WordWake only show one feed on Free?

The free tier is the Language feed in one language, for the first 3 stages. Once you finish stage 3, you can either keep practicing those stages or upgrade to WordWake Plus for stages 4 through 101.

### Can I change my language after starting?

Yes, in **Settings → Language**. Your progress on the previous language is saved if you switch back later. With Plus, you can switch between any of the 5 languages at any time.

### What is the stage check?

It's an optional 10-question readiness check. If you pass with 8 or more correct, the app moves you to the next stage. If you don't pass, your current stage stays in rotation until tomorrow. This exists so cards don't get harder before they feel familiar.

### My widget isn't updating.

Try these in order:

1. Make sure WordWake is open and you've used the app at least once recently — this primes the widget cache.
2. Check **Settings → General → Background App Refresh** and confirm it's enabled for WordWake.
3. Remove the widget from your Lock Screen, then add it again.
4. If it still doesn't work, email us at **hello@magnetmates.app** with your iPhone model and iOS version.

### My progress disappeared.

Progress is stored locally on your device. The most common causes of lost progress are:

- Deleting and reinstalling the app
- Restoring your iPhone from an older backup
- Signing in to a different Apple ID

If your progress vanished after one of these, it cannot usually be recovered. We're sorry — we don't have a server-side backup because we deliberately don't store user data on our servers.

## Content questions

### Some words on my cards seem regional or outdated.

Spanish, French, and Italian all have meaningful regional variation. We aim for neutral, broadly understood vocabulary, but if you see a card that feels wrong for your region, please email us with the card and what you'd expect instead. We use this feedback to improve the content.

### Can I request a new language?

Yes — email **hello@magnetmates.app** with the language you'd like to see. We track requests and use them to prioritize future content.

### Can I suggest new cards or content categories?

Absolutely. Email **hello@magnetmates.app**. We can't promise we'll add everything, but we read all suggestions.

## Privacy

### What does WordWake collect about me?

Nothing. We have no user accounts, no servers that store user data, and no third-party analytics. Your progress and settings live on your device. See our full [Privacy Policy](./privacy) for details.
